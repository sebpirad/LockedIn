# Rejected candidates — explorer category

One line each: quote start · attributed person · reason. "QI" = Quote Investigator. "Spare" lines are genuine but were left out because of the two-per-person cap.

- "Men wanted for hazardous journey…" · Ernest Shackleton · Known fabrication (per the brief): no copy of the newspaper advertisement has ever been found. Not re-checked.
- "Superhuman effort isn't worth a damn unless it achieves results" · Ernest Shackleton · Not in the full text of South (1919); no primary source located.
- "Optimism is true moral courage" · Ernest Shackleton · Not in the full text of South (1919); no primary source located.
- "It is not the mountain we conquer but ourselves" · Edmund Hillary · QI (2016) concludes that Hillary did not originate it; the saying was reassigned to him and he later adopted it.
- "The difficult is what takes a little time; the impossible…" · Fridtjof Nansen · QI (2015): an older saying. Nansen used a version in 1925 at the League of Nations but did not claim it as his own.
- "Never stop because you are afraid — you are never so likely to be wrong…" · Fridtjof Nansen · Not found in the 1927 Hogarth text of his St Andrews address, where it is usually sourced. The verified "line of retreat" sentence (x02) is used instead.
- "The most difficult thing is the decision to act, the rest is merely tenacity" · Amelia Earhart · Not in 20 Hrs. 40 Min. (1928); no QI article; no primary source located.
- "The most effective way to do it, is to do it" · Amelia Earhart · Not in 20 Hrs. 40 Min.; no primary source located.
- "Please know I am quite aware of the hazards…" · Amelia Earhart · Probably genuine (letter printed in Last Flight, 1937), but the only scans are lending-restricted, so the wording could not be checked.
- "Because it's there" · George Mallory · Only a newspaper's report of an interview (New York Times, 18 March 1923), not his own text, and the authenticity is debated. No QI verdict. Not used.
- "I will go anywhere, provided it be forward" · David Livingstone · Blaikie's Personal Life of David Livingstone (1880) quotes only the fragment "to go anywhere — provided it be FORWARD"; the popular full sentence is a paraphrase.
- "Knowledge begets knowledge…" · John Glenn · Could not find a primary text of his 26 February 1962 address to Congress.
- "All adventures, especially into new territory, are scary" · Sally Ride · Not in either of her NASA JSC oral histories (2002); no primary source located. x12 is used instead.
- "Never be limited by other people's limited imaginations" / "Don't let anyone rob you of your imagination…" · Mae Jemison · Not in the Scholastic interview; no primary transcript located.
- "Поехали!" / "Эй, небо, сними шляпу!" · Yuri Gagarin / Valentina Tereshkova · No named published English translation could be tied to a checked original in this pass. Not researched further.
- "I said I could and I would. And I did." · Nellie Bly · Spare: genuine (Ten Days in a Mad-House, 1887, Ch. I; Project Gutenberg #59899).
- "Had we lived, I should have had a tale to tell…" · Robert Falcon Scott · Spare: genuine ("Message to the Public", Gutenberg #11579), 29 words.
- "In memories we were rich. We had pierced the veneer of outside things." · Ernest Shackleton · Spare: genuine (South, Ch. X), but off-theme.
- "And I tell you, if you have the desire for knowledge and the power to give it physical expression, go out and explore." · Apsley Cherry-Garrard · Spare: genuine (The Worst Journey in the World, Ch. XIX).
