# Rejected candidates — entrepreneur category

One line each: quote start · attributed person · reason. "QI" = Quote Investigator. Where a genuine line by the same person was used instead, the id is given.

- "Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish." · Steve Jobs · Genuinely in the Stanford 2005 speech, but Jobs is quoting the farewell message of the Whole Earth Catalog (Stewart Brand et al.), not his own words. Used e01/e02 instead.
- "When something is important enough, you do it even if the odds are not in your favor." · Elon Musk · Paraphrase. The CBS 60 Minutes transcript (30 Mar 2014) has different words; that exact wording is used as e03.
- "Whether you think you can, or you think you can't — you're right." · Henry Ford · Not in My Life and Work (full text searched); only later, second-hand attributions.
- "If I had asked people what they wanted, they would have said faster horses." · Henry Ford · Not in My Life and Work; QI (2011, "My Customers Would Have Asked For a Faster Horse") finds no evidence Ford said it.
- "Thinking is the hardest work there is, which is probably the reason why so few engage in it." · Henry Ford · Paraphrase; the book's own wording is used as e06.
- "If you can dream it, you can do it." · Walt Disney · No source from Disney's lifetime found; commonly traced to the script of EPCOT's Horizons attraction (opened 1983, 17 years after his death) — that origin not checked by me against a primary source. Used e07 (QI-confirmed) instead.
- "I never dreamed about success. I worked for it." · Estée Lauder · Not in her autobiography Estée: A Success Story (1985; full text searched); only in quote collections. Used e19 instead.
- "I didn't get there by wishing for it or hoping for it, but by working for it." · Estée Lauder · Not in her autobiography (full text searched); appears only in quote books and magazines without a source.
- "I am a woman who came from the cotton fields of the South. From there I was promoted to the washtub…" · Madam C. J. Walker · Smoothed modern version; the 1912 convention report wording (via the National Park Service lesson) is used as e11.
- "Don't sit down and wait for the opportunities to come. Get up and make them." · Madam C. J. Walker · Only traceable to a 1991 juvenile biography (A'Lelia Bundles, p. 105) with no original date or document; not used.
- "There is no royal flower-strewn path to success…" / "I got my start by giving myself a start." · Madam C. J. Walker · No primary source located (Wikiquote cites only quote websites).
- "To succeed you have to believe in something with such a passion that it becomes a reality." · Anita Roddick · Quote IS genuine (Body and Soul, 1991, matched in full text), but Commons has no usable free portrait of her (only a group photo with children), so the person was dropped.
- "If you think you're too small to have an impact, try going to bed with a mosquito." · Anita Roddick · Proverb-like quip found in many quote books before/independently of her; no Roddick primary source located. (Person dropped anyway — no portrait.)
