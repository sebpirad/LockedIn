# Rejected candidates — athlete category

One line each: quote start · attributed person · reason. "QI" = Quote Investigator. "IA full text" = the Internet Archive's full-text index of scanned books, newspapers and magazines, searched for the exact phrase; "only in quotation anthologies" means every hit there is a quote collection or yearbook with no original occasion.
